I’m also a spontaneous person who likes trying new things and making unexpected memories.

One thing about me: I can tolerate a lot of things, but please learn how to open a toothpaste sachet properly. 😭 And if you're constantly negative or pessimistic, we might have a problem. I like being around people who bring positive energy and believe that there is always something worth looking forward to.

MY GOALS

I’ve always aspired to become a successful physician here in the Philippines. But beyond the title or career, my biggest motivation is my family—especially my parents. One of my ultimate goals is to reach a point where I can give them whatever they want and let them enjoy the life they deserve.

At the same time, I want to become a nurse with a genuine heart for service—someone who can make a difference in the lives of patients, even through small acts of care and compassion.

Of course, I also want to be financially successful, travel around the world, experience different cultures, and enjoy the life I worked hard for. I want to see places I've only seen in pictures and make memories along the way.

And after all the dreams, titles, money, and achievements, my simplest goal is probably the most important one: I just want to be happy. Not the perfect kind of happy, but the kind where I can look around one day and feel that everything I worked for was worth it #RN2028.
